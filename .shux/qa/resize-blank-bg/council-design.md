# Design council for #295 (held after implementation)

This council ran after the fix was implemented, on 7cff347: #295 began as a defect found while gating #190 B1 and went straight to an implementation council and three closure rounds. Its blockers were fixed red-first afterwards; its provenance-policy recommendation is documented in the PR as out of scope.

Agents: codex, agy (dootsabha council). Synthesis:

Ārya, **request changes on shux #295 (`f1478e4..7cff347`)**. Keep the primary reflow and alternate fixed-canvas design. Fix the cursor overwrite and the viewport boundary calculation, replace the wide-gap sentinel, and make the printed-space policy explicit before merging. The cross-review reports that all 455 `shux-vt` library tests passed and that targeted probes reproduced several of the failures below. [shux #295](https://github.com/indrasvat/shux/issues/295)

| Priority | Decision | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| Merge blocker | **Preserve cursor intent across reflow** | At width 10, printing `ABCDE `, shrinking to 5, and typing `F` produced `ABCDF`. Widening again before typing still left the cursor on `E`. Preserve the insertion position after a printed separator and keep explicitly addressed columns on the coordinate path. Dropping `extra == 0` would also arm pending wrap for a `CUP` into trailing padding. The current cursor state can already make printing and `CUP` indistinguishable at an exact edge, so cover both paths in the regression. [Offset mapping](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1665) |
| Merge blocker | **Use the reflowed history boundary for viewport trimming** | Keep the live primary cursor or the `?1049`-parked cursor as the only row pin. The trim floor currently uses the old scrollback count. A widen with a simultaneous height reduction moved `TOP` into history even though a blank row below the cursor could yield. The source review also identified the reverse case, where history expands on a width shrink and can displace visible rows. Cover both directions. [History floor](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1608) |
| Merge blocker | **Give wide-glyph gaps dedicated cell metadata** | The `Arc` sentinel makes `extended.is_some()` true on a synthetic gap, and `prune_default_extended()` was shown to erase the marker. Store the gap in an internal bit, keep it out of visual equality and frame capture, and preserve it by value in any future format that restores a live grid. One probe measured a metadata-byte layout at the same 24 bytes as the current `Cell` on that compiler. Check cross-crate constructors and the reported `DCH` case, where deleting before a gap may leave an extra space after widening. [Cell representation](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/cell.rs:87) · [Character deletion](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1859) |

**For blank cells, track provenance and choose survival separately from wrapping.** The current style test drops background-only printed spaces and can wrap spaces that carry only foreground or invisible styling. Both behaviors were reproduced, including a `10 → 11` widen that lost four blue cells. My recommendation is to let explicitly printed spaces with visible paint or an OSC 8 target contribute to reflow, trim trailing invisible padding, and preserve the cursor’s insertion position independently. Keep storage elision strict so surviving interior cells retain their attributes. Trailing background-color erase fill should keep what fits on the final physical row and yield below the cursor. [Reflow reader](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:183) · [Padding classification](/private/tmp/vt-design/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:212)

That is a policy choice beyond #295’s surviving-cell guarantee. Reflowing every printed space would also preserve invisible whitespace, at the cost of blank continuation rows from padded prompts and fixed-width CLI output. Kitty supports distinguishing printed spaces from erased cells and discards trailing erase fill during reflow. Alacritty treats a nondefault foreground as occupied, and WezTerm can trim literal trailing spaces on mixed text lines. The upstream implementations support different tradeoffs. [Kitty resize](/https://github.com/kovidgoyal/kitty/blob/master/kitty/resize.c) · [Alacritty cell occupancy](https://github.com/alacritty/alacritty/blob/master/alacritty_terminal/src/term/cell.rs) · [WezTerm wrapping](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm/blob/main/wezterm-surface/src/line/line.rs)

Keep the DECAWM-gated resize rule and the existing DECSC save/restore behavior with screen provenance. The parser’s pending-wrap behavior when DECAWM is toggled is a separate reproduced inconsistency, and a parser fix needs to account for grapheme lookup. For `?1047`, the stashed primary already uses the same resize path. Horizontal color loss comes from padding clipping, while the missing parked cursor affects vertical trimming. Verify alternate-screen trimming with an OSC 8 linked blank as a separate case. The reported one-column wide-glyph offset issue needs a reachability check because normal shux resize clamps widths to at least two columns.
