# Implementation council for #295 (on 73e9de4)

Agents: codex, agy (dootsabha council). Synthesis:

**Verdict: FIX before merging `73e9de4`.** The confirmed blocker is text eviction during column shrink. At 10 columns × 6 rows, clear the screen with a blue background, print `ABCDEFGH` at the top, then shrink to 4 columns. The parent keeps `ABCD` and `EFGH` visible. The commit keeps all five erase-filled rows, pushing `ABCD` into scrollback. With `max_scrollback = 0`, it permanently evicts `ABCD`. The mapped cursor can also be clamped onto a different row when scrollback is available. [grid.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1533)

### Changes for this commit

1. **Keep erase fill from displacing text.** Select the viewport around the mapped cursor before enforcing the history cap. Let excess `EL`/`ED` fill below the cursor yield when text reflows, while preserving the background of erased cells that stay visible. Explicitly printed blank rows can be meaningful output, so background color alone is insufficient to decide what to discard. Cover both `EL` and `ED`, with zero and nonzero scrollback. [grid.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1533) · [parser.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/parser.rs:1430)

2. **Define how printed blanks and extended attributes survive reflow.** The commit retains one of five blue spaces on a 10 → 11 column widen, compared with zero on the parent. A 10 → 9 → 10 round trip retains 4 → 4 → 2. Both results arise because a soft-wrapped row contributes all its cells, while the hard tail clips its trailing spaces. If printed styled or linked spaces count as logical content, reflow needs to distinguish them from erase fill and implicit padding. If the commit keeps its stated no-wrap policy for trailing blanks, specify how clipping applies across soft wraps and test widening as well as shrinking. The retention check also needs to account for extended attributes such as OSC 8 hyperlinks. A global change to `Row::is_blank()` would affect height-only resize and text capture too. [grid.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1482) · [grid.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:418) · [lib.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/lib.rs:1495)

3. **Skip copying default padding.** Copy only the suffix span needed to preserve attributes and keep cursor offsets independent of that span. The 5,064-row replay staged roughly 28 MB of suffix cells. One debug A/B measured 29 → 59 ms for resize and 71.7 → 71.9 MB peak RSS. That supports fixing the redundant work, while the claimed 24 MB peak RSS increase remains unverified. Add a cursor assertion too. Default padding can leave cell runs identical while changing serialized cursor coordinates after either a printed `$ ` or explicit cursor movement into untouched padding. [grid.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/grid.rs:1504) · [capture.rs](/private/tmp/vt-council/crates/shux-vt/src/capture.rs:641)
