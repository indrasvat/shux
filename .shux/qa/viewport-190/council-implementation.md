### Inherited issues and spec gap

- **Wheel routing.** #190 treats app-forwarded wheel input as committed input. The B1 design also calls for preserving existing wheel policy, so settle that scope explicitly. The real wheel handler skips driver promotion, and alternate-scroll arrows bypass the stamped-cell check. If #190’s committed-input rule applies in B1, distinguish consumed events from successfully queued PTY input and test driver election for forwarded, surplus, and evicted-stamp events. Verify that the neighboring pane cell survives and that yank matches what was shown.
